Local vendor discovery app — full product plan including features, user flows, screen mockups, and roadmap

Core concept
For customers
Search for an item → see which nearby stores carry it → get directions or call directly. No more wandering around asking.
For vendors
List your store and products in under 5 minutes. No tech knowledge needed. WhatsApp-style simplicity.
Feature set — customer app
🔍 Item-first search
Search "Amul butter 500g" or "Allen Solly white shirt XL" and get a map of stores that carry it nearby.
Core
📍 Explore by map
Browse stores around you on a live map. Filter by category: grocery, electronics, clothing, pharmacy, etc.
Core
🏪 Store profiles
Full store page: name, location, open hours, photos, product catalog with prices, WhatsApp/call button.
Core
💬 Ask the store
One-tap WhatsApp integration. Customer asks "do you have X in size Y?" without leaving the app.
Phase 2
🔔 Stock alerts
Notify me when a specific item is back in stock at a nearby store.
Phase 2
🌐 Multilingual
Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Bengali support. Voice search in local language.
Phase 2
⭐ Reviews + photos
Leave a review. Upload a photo of the store. Community-verified inventory.
Phase 3
🤖 AI assistant
"Where can I find a blue kurti under ₹500 near Dadar?" — natural language store finder.
Phase 3
Feature set — vendor app
📦 Product listing
Photo + name + price + stock status. Bulk upload via CSV or WhatsApp image. As easy as posting on Instagram.
Core
📊 Footfall insights
See how many people viewed your store, called, got directions. Which products get the most searches.

📣 Local promotions

Monetization
Free for vendors (always)
Basic listing, up to 50 products, analytics. Must stay free to get critical mass of small vendors onboarded.
Vendor Pro — ₹199–499/month
Unlimited products, priority ranking in search, promotional banners, WhatsApp catalog sync, advanced analytics.
Boosted listings (pay-per-day)
Vendors pay ₹49–99/day to appear at the top of category results in their area. Low-friction upsell.
Hyperlocal ads
Businesses with multiple branches pay for area-specific promotion. Think pharmacy chains, clothing labels.
Launch roadmap
1
MVP — 1 neighbourhood, 1 city
Map view, search by item, store profiles, vendor onboarding. Manually onboard 50–100 stores yourself. Validate the "search by item" use case.
2
Growth — city-wide, self-serve onboarding
WhatsApp catalog sync, multilingual support, stock alerts, vendor Pro tier, local-area ambassadors to onboard vendors.
3
Scale — multi-city, AI features
AI natural language search, voice in regional languages, community-verified product data, hyperlocal ad platform.
Your real moat vs magicpin
Vendor simplicity
Magicpin requires effort. Your onboarding should feel like Instagram. Photo + product name + done.
Item-first discovery
Nobody lets you search "Allen Solly white shirt size L" and get a map of stores. That specific UX is wide open.
Visitor / tourist mode
Explicitly design for "I just landed in this city and need X." Nobody targets this use case.
No delivery dependency
You don't handle logistics. You're a discovery layer, not a Blinkit. Zero operational overhead.
